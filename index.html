Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Social Publisher application uses information obtained through Meta APIs (Facebook Graph API and Instagram Graph API).

Information We Access

Social Publisher only accesses information necessary to publish and manage content on Facebook Pages and Instagram Professional accounts that are explicitly authorized by the account owner.

This may include:

  • Facebook Page ID
  • Instagram Professional Account ID
  • Page and account names
  • Access Tokens provided by Meta
  • Content submitted by the user for publishing

How We Use Information

The information is used solely to:

  • Publish posts to Facebook Pages.
  • Publish posts to Instagram Professional accounts.
  • Manage content authorized by the account owner.
  • Maintain application functionality.

Data Sharing

Social Publisher does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Data Storage

Access tokens and related credentials are stored securely and are only used to perform actions requested by the authorized account owner.

User Control

Users can revoke the application's access at any time through their Facebook or Instagram account settings.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of stored data by contacting us using the email below. Upon request, associated data will be permanently removed unless retention is required by law.
